ReThank you, and goodbye

To the SOC team, analysts and managers,

For two and a half years my job was to stand between your work and the customers who depend on it. The detecting, the investigating and the calls at difficult hours were yours. I was lucky to stand that close to it.

To the analysts: thank you for teaching me what good looks like from the inside, and for never treating an alert as just another ticket. To the managers: thank you for the trust, the straight talk and for sharing the load when it got heavy.

I am leaving, but I am taking what you taught me with me. Keep escalating early. Keep covering for each other. Keep being the reason our customers sleep well.
